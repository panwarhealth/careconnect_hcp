Privacy Policy

  1. 1. Introduction

    1. 1.1
      Care Pharmaceuticals Pty Limited (“CP”, “we”, “our”, “us”) respects and upholds your rights to privacy protection under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) ("Privacy Act") and is required to comply with the Australian Privacy Principles in respect of the collection, use, disclosure and handling of personal information. This policy details the practices we have adopted to protect your privacy, so that you can feel confident about how we manage the personal information you entrust to us.
    2. 1.2
      By providing us with your personal information, you consent to CP using and disclosing your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.
    3. 1.3
      Under the Privacy Act, personal information means information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable, whether the information or opinion is true or not and whether the information or opinion is recorded in a material form or not.
    4. 1.4
      Personal information includes sensitive information, which is defined under the Privacy Act as information or an opinion about a person's race, ethnic origin, political opinions, membership of political associations and trade associations, religious or philosophical beliefs, sexual orientation or practices, criminal record, health information, genetic information about an individual that is not otherwise health information, biometric information that is used for the purpose of automated biometric verification or biometric identification and biometric templates.

  2. 2. When and how do we collect personal information?

    1. 2.1
      We collect personal information from healthcare professionals (HCPs) for various business and other purposes further listed below. In this section, we explain the types of personal information we usually collect as well as how we usually collect this information.
    2. 2.2
      The types of personal information we usually collect and hold will depend on the circumstances in which that information is collected. It may include:
      1. (a)
        contact details, including name, e-mail, phone number and postal address;
      2. (b)
        age and gender;
      3. (c)
        professional registration details, including Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA) registration number, Royal Australian College of General Practitioners (RACGP) registration number, profession type, profession number, country of practice and year of registration;
      4. (d)
        practice details, including title and specialty;
      5. (e)
        qualification details, including qualification title, awarding institution, country and year obtained;
      6. (f)
        statistical information regarding the use of our website, including website users’ IP addresses and the dates and times of visits; and
      7. (g)
        other personal information that we require or that you volunteer to us.
    3. 2.3
      We usually collect personal information in the following ways:
      1. (a)
        through our website, including via the “Contact Us” section;
      2. (b)
        when you complete a registration form;
      3. (c)
        publicly available sources of information (such as conferences, telephone directories or social media);
      4. (d)
        government agencies; and
      5. (e)
        through third party service providers, including third party service providers who provide and house training and educational materials for HCPs and other persons.
    4. 2.4
      If you do not provide us with the information we request, we may not be able to fulfil the applicable purpose of collection, such as to supply products or services to you.
    5. 2.5
      You may have the option of not identifying yourself, or of using a pseudonym, when dealing with us, unless it is legally necessary or impracticable for us to deal with individuals who are not identified. If we request your personal information but would prefer to remain anonymous, please let us know. We will notify you if we require you to be correctly identified for a particular interaction.
    6. 2.6
      Where practicable, we will collect personal information directly from you. If we receive information about you from someone else (for example from someone who supplies goods or services to us), we will take reasonable steps to ensure you are aware that we have collected personal information about you and the circumstances of the collection.

      Sensitive Information

    7. 2.7
      We may collect sensitive information directly from you, for example information regarding your membership of a professional association.
    8. 2.8
      Unless otherwise required or permitted by law, we will only collect, hold, use or disclose your sensitive information with your consent and where it is reasonably necessary to provide our services or perform our functions.

  3. 3. What do we do with the personal information?

    1. 3.1
      CP’s primary purposes in collecting, holding, using and disclosing personal information are to:
      1. (a)
        provide products and services to HCPs;
      2. (b)
        communicate with HCPs, including producing information you request, responding to your enquiries, managing complaints, or otherwise facilitating the purpose for which you have contacted us;
      3. (c)
        handle payments;
      4. (d)
        verify your identity, if required;
      5. (e)
        market our products and services;
      6. (f)
        send you material on our activities, products and services that we believe may be of interest to you;
      7. (g)
        send invitations to events;
      8. (h)
        inform you about continuing education activities and courses;
      9. (i)
        seek feedback from you and perform market research, so that we can gauge your satisfaction with our products or services;
      10. (j)
        generally carry on our business (including maintaining our business records and ensuring compliance with our legal and insurance obligations);
      11. (k)
        administer training programs or other similar programs in which HCPs agree to be involved;
      12. (l)
        to engage in other activities where required or permitted by law or where you have given your consent; and
      13. (m)
        to share it with Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to assist us with updates to our website and the products and services that we may offer to you.
    2. 3.2
      Your personal information is never shared, sold, rented or otherwise disclosed outside CP (except as required for any statutory or legal requirements) in ways different from what is described in this Privacy Policy, unless you have consented to such different use or disclosure, or such use or disclosure is otherwise required or permitted by law, or where your consent may be reasonably inferred from the circumstances.

  4. 4. Cookies

    1. 4.1
      Like many companies, we sometimes use “cookie” technology on our websites. These cookies are stored on your computer by your browser. When you log in, the cookie tells us whether you’ve visited us before or you are a new visitor. The cookie does obtain personal information about a HCP including a HCP’s username, email address and/or AHPRA number. The cookie doesn’t extract any information from your computer. We use the cookie to help us identify website features in which you have the greatest interest, so that we can provide more of what you want. Our website also sets third-party analytics cookies (Google Analytics, and Microsoft Clarity is planned), though there is no identifiable information with these cookies.

  5. 5. Tracking pixels

    1. 5.1
      We may collect and use third party tracking pixels to collect personal information about a HCP including a HCP’s username, email address, AHPRA number, the HCP’s activity on our website and in relation to our digital services including emails we send, transaction data such as items viewed, network information such as IP address and geolocation data, and URL information.
    2. 5.2
      We use this information to understand your activity and use of our website in order to help us identify website features in which you have the greatest interest, so that we can provide more of what you want and/or assist us in any future promotional, marketing, market research, product development and publicity purposes.
    3. 5.3
      Any personal information which is obtained via third party tracking pixels may be disclosed to those third-party providers who may operate outside Australia. Disclosure of personal information is set out in this Privacy Policy.

  6. 6. Technical, usage and marketing information

    1. 6.1
      Our website captures certain technical, usage and marketing metadata including when you complete a registration form. The captured metadata includes your approximate geolocation, referral source, landing page and internal customer relationship management or segmentation data.
    2. 6.2
      We do not attempt to identify individuals using this information, and only use it for statistical analysis, system administration, and similar related purposes.
    3. 6.3
      All unidentifiable information you provide us is disclosed to Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity for analytic purposes.
    4. 6.4
      We will generate reports from aggregated, deidentified user activity we have collected from our website for platform improvement, content strategy and educational outcomes research. We may disclose these reports to continuing education providers at our discretion.

  7. 7. Links

    1. 7.1
      Websites operated by CP may contain links to other sites operated by third parties, including third party service providers who house and provide training and educational materials for HCPs. CP makes no representations or warranties as to the privacy practices of any third-party site and is not responsible for the privacy practices of the third parties that operate those sites.

  1. 8. Direct Marketing

    1. 8.1
      If you have provided your consent for us to do so, your personal information may be used for future promotional, marketing, market research, product development and publicity purposes. If you are receiving such material from us, and do not wish to receive it any longer, you can opt-out by using the unsubscribe facilities included in our marketing communications, or by contacting CP at the details provided in the section below entitled “CP contact details” and requesting your removal from our mailing lists.

  2. 9. Disclosure of personal information

    1. 9.1
      We may disclose, and you consent to our disclosure, of your personal information:

      (i) within CP;
      (ii) to our related companies and affiliates in Australia or outside of Australia; and (iii) to third parties who assist us in providing our products, services, educational and training programs, including to third party continuing education providers, and related activities to HCPs or other persons. This may include disclosure to:

      1. (a)
        AHPRA;
      2. (b)
        RACGP;
      3. (c)
        service providers that support communications, technology, logistics, verification, accreditation, education and continuing education, reporting, analytics and other business operations;
      4. (d)
        cloud-based services that host CP data (including data comprising your personal information) on it services;
      5. (e)
        authorised representatives of CP, such as our lawyers and accountants; and
      6. (f)
        third party service providers who assist us with hosting, analytics, platform operation and platform security matters.
    2. 9.2
      Where we disclose your personal information to third parties, we take reasonable steps to ensure they are subject to appropriate obligations to protect the privacy, confidentiality and security of your personal information and to use it only for the purposes for which it was disclosed.

  3. 10. Disclosure of personal information outside Australia

    1. 10.1
      We may disclose personal information to our related companies and affiliates within and outside of Australia, as well as to service providers located outside of Australia, including New Zealand and the USA. The privacy laws of those countries may not provide the same level of protection as the Privacy Act. However, we will only disclose personal information to overseas recipients with your consent, where we have taken reasonable steps to ensure that the overseas recipient does not breach the Australian Privacy Principles in relation to the information, or if we are required to or permitted to do so by law.

  4. 11. Security of your personal information

    1. 11.1
      Any personal information you provide to us may be kept in hard copy format and stored on cloud-based systems hosted in Australia.
    2. 11.2
      We will endeavour to take all reasonable steps to keep secure any personal information which we hold about you, and to protect this information from misuse, interference and loss as well as unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. These steps include the use of computer access passwords, lock-up cabinets, personnel policies and firewalls.
    3. 11.3
      We require our employees and data processors to respect the confidentiality of any personal information held by us.
    4. 11.4
      However, we cannot and do not guarantee that personal information we hold will be protected against unauthorised access or misuse.

  5. 12. Retention of information

    1. 12.1
      When we no longer need to use your information, we will take reasonable steps to properly de-identify or destroy it. We may retain your personal information where we are required or permitted to do so by law, such as for insurance or legal reasons.
    2. 12.2
      If you wish to have your personal information deleted, please contact CP using the details provided in the section below entitled “CP contact details” and we will take all reasonable steps to delete it unless we are required or permitted to keep it by law.

  6. 13. Can you access and correct personal information that you have submitted to us?

    1. 13.1
      We aim to take reasonable steps to ensure that the personal information we collect, use and disclose is accurate, up-to-date and complete.
    2. 13.2
      You may contact us to request access to or correction of the personal information we hold about you. Please contact CP using the details provided in the section below entitled “CP contact details”.
    3. 13.3
      We may refuse to allow access or to amend your personal information if we are legally required or permitted to do so. In that case, we will (unless it is unreasonable to do so) provide you with written reasons for the refusal together with information about the options available to complain about the refusal.
    4. 13.4
      We will respond to your request within a reasonable period after the request is made and we will give access to the information in the manner requested if it is reasonable and practicable to do so. We may require you to comply with certain procedures before we allow access to or amendment of your personal information (e.g., providing satisfactory identification), in order to ensure the integrity and security of information that we hold. Please understand that our requirements to identify individuals requesting access to personal information are designed to protect you and other individuals from unauthorised access.
    5. 13.5
      We may charge you a small fee to cover any administrative costs that we may incur in providing personal information to you. However, we will not charge a fee for you to lodge a request for access to or correction of your personal information.

  7. 14. Complaints about privacy

    1. 14.1
      If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, or any complaint regarding treatment of your privacy by us, please contact CP at the details provided in the section below entitled “CP contact details”.
    2. 14.2
      In most cases we will ask that you put your request in writing to us. We will investigate your complaint and will use reasonable endeavours to respond to you in writing within 30 days of receiving the written complaint. If you are dissatisfied with the response that you receive from us, you have the right to make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (https://www.oaic.gov.au/).
